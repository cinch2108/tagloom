Product Schema Generator with Offers & Ratings

How it works

Create Product structured data with name, images, brand, SKU, GTIN, an Offer with price, currency, availability and condition, and an AggregateRating with average score and review count. It's the markup that makes product pages eligible for price, stock and star snippets.

How to use it

  1. Enter the product name, image and description.
  2. Add brand, SKU and GTIN if you have them.
  3. Fill in price, currency and availability.
  4. Add your average rating and review count if they're shown on the page.
  5. Copy the JSON-LD and test it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need reviews for product rich results?

No. Google needs at least one of offers, review or aggregateRating, so a price and availability is enough.

Can I add ratings from another site?

No. Ratings must come from reviews shown on your own page, or Google may ignore them or take manual action.

What currency format should I use?

A three-letter ISO 4217 code such as USD, EUR or GBP, and a plain number for the price, like 29.99.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
