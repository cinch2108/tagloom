Apple Touch Icon Generator (180 × 180 PNG)

How it works

Create the 180 × 180 apple-touch-icon.png that iPhones and iPads use when someone adds your site to the home screen. Tagloom fills the background so iOS doesn't turn transparent areas black, adds padding so your logo isn't cramped, and includes the full favicon pack in case you need it.

How to use it

  1. Choose your logo image.
  2. Add padding and a background color.
  3. Check the home-screen preview.
  4. Download the ZIP or just the PNG you need.
  5. Add the apple-touch-icon link tag to your <head>.

Frequently asked questions

What size is an Apple touch icon?

180 × 180 pixels covers all current iPhones and iPads; iOS scales it down where needed.

Do I need to round the corners?

No. iOS applies its own rounded mask, so upload a square icon.

Where should the file go?

Put apple-touch-icon.png in your site root and link it with <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
