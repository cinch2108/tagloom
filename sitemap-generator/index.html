XML Sitemap Generator from a URL List

How it works

Paste or load a list of URLs and Tagloom writes a valid sitemap.xml following the sitemaps.org protocol. It removes duplicates and #fragments, rejects invalid or non-http URLs, escapes special characters like &, adds optional lastmod, changefreq and priority values, and splits large lists into multiple files with a sitemap index.

How to use it

  1. Paste your URLs, one per line, or load a .txt or .csv file.
  2. Set a base URL if your list has relative paths.
  3. Choose lastmod, changefreq and priority options.
  4. Fix any invalid lines listed in the report.
  5. Download sitemap.xml and submit it in Google Search Console.

Frequently asked questions

How many URLs can one sitemap hold?

Up to 50,000 URLs and 50 MB uncompressed. Tagloom splits bigger lists automatically and adds a sitemap index.

Does Google use priority and changefreq?

Google ignores both. It does use lastmod when it is accurate, so only include it if your dates are real.

Which URLs should I include?

Only canonical, indexable pages that return 200. Leave out redirects, noindex pages and duplicates.

How do I tell Google about my sitemap?

Submit it in Search Console and add a Sitemap: line to your robots.txt.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
