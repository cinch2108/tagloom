On-Page SEO Checker for Your Target Keyword

How it works

Paste a page's HTML and enter your target keyword. Tagloom checks whether it appears in the title tag, the meta description, the H1 and the first paragraph, measures its density, flags multiple H1s, skipped heading levels, images without alt text and thin content, and shows the heading outline.

How to use it

  1. Open your page, view its source and copy the HTML.
  2. Paste it into the box and enter your keyword.
  3. Read the checklist of passes and warnings.
  4. Review the headings outline.
  5. Fix the issues and run the check again.

Frequently asked questions

Where should my keyword appear?

In the title tag, the H1, the first paragraph and naturally in the body and a few subheadings.

Is it safe to paste HTML with scripts?

Yes. The HTML is parsed into an inert document with scripts removed; nothing is executed or added to this page.

How many H1 tags should a page have?

One is best. It should describe the page's main topic and usually matches the title closely.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
