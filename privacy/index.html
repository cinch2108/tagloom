Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-25.

What you enter stays on your device

Every tool runs in your browser. We never receive, store or see the titles, descriptions, URLs, robots rules, URL lists, images, HTML, text or server settings you enter. Images you pick for previews and favicons are read locally and never uploaded, and pasted HTML is parsed in an inert document that never runs. Preferences like your theme and recently used tools are saved only in your browser's local storage, and you can clear them at any time.

Advertising and cookies

Tagloom is free because of ads. If you accept cookies, our advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies to show more relevant ads and measure them. If you choose "Only necessary," only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't use accounts, sign-ups or analytics that track you.
  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't fetch, crawl or upload anything you type or choose in the tools.

Your rights

Under laws like the GDPR and CCPA, you can opt out of personalized ads at any time. Because we don't store your personal data, there's nothing for us to delete, but you can contact us at hello@example.com with any questions.
