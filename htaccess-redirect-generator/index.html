Htaccess Redirect Generator: 301, HTTPS and WWW

How it works

Generate an Apache .htaccess file with 301 and 302 redirects, forced HTTPS, www or non-www, trailing-slash rules, a custom 404 page, gzip compression, browser caching and security headers. Every redirect line is validated so you don't upload a typo that breaks your site, and loops and chains are flagged.

How to use it

  1. Enter your domain.
  2. Choose HTTPS, www and trailing-slash options.
  3. Add redirects as “/old /new 301”, one per line.
  4. Pick compression, caching and security headers.
  5. Download .htaccess, test it on staging, then upload it to your web root.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between 301 and 302?

A 301 is permanent and passes ranking signals to the new URL. A 302 is temporary and tells search engines to keep the old URL indexed.

Should I use www or non-www?

Either is fine for SEO. Pick one, redirect the other to it, and use it in your canonical tags and sitemap.

Why isn't my .htaccess working?

Your host must run Apache (or LiteSpeed) with mod_rewrite and AllowOverride enabled. nginx ignores .htaccess files.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
