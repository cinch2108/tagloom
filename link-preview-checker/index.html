Link Preview Checker for Social and Chat Apps

How it works

Compare approximate link previews side by side in generic styles inspired by Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Slack and Discord, all from one set of tags. It's a quick way to see whether your title is too long, your description gets cut, or your image crops badly in a square layout.

How to use it

  1. Enter your page title, description and URL.
  2. Pick your share image from your device to preview it.
  3. Review all five previews at once.
  4. Fix warnings like relative URLs or a wrong image ratio.
  5. Copy the og: and twitter: tags.

Frequently asked questions

Why do apps show different previews?

Each app reads the same Open Graph tags but crops images and truncates text differently.

Can this fetch my live page?

No. Tagloom never makes network requests for you, which keeps it private. Enter your tags or copy them from your page.

Why does Slack show a small image?

Slack and some chat apps show a small thumbnail when the image is square or below the size they need for a large preview.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
