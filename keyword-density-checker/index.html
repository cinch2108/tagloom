Keyword Density Checker: 1, 2 and 3-Word Phrases

How it works

Paste text or HTML and Tagloom counts the most frequent one-, two- and three-word phrases with their density, after filtering common stopwords. You also get word, sentence and character counts, reading time, a headings outline and checks for a target keyword. HTML is parsed safely in an inert document, so scripts never run.

How to use it

  1. Paste your article text or the page's HTML source.
  2. Type the keyword you want to rank for.
  3. Review the 1, 2 and 3-word phrase tables.
  4. Check the density and placement warnings.
  5. Rewrite overused phrases with synonyms.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good keyword density?

There's no magic number. Most well-ranked pages land around 0.5–2%. Above about 3% the text often reads as stuffed.

Why filter stopwords?

Words like “the” and “and” are always the most common, so hiding them shows the phrases that actually describe your topic.

How is reading time calculated?

Words divided by 238, the average adult silent reading speed in words per minute.

Does Tagloom fetch my URL?

No. Paste the text or HTML yourself; nothing is fetched or uploaded.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
