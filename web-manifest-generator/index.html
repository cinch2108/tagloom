Web App Manifest Generator with PWA Icons

How it works

Generate a site.webmanifest with your app name, short name, theme color, background color and the 192, 512 and maskable 512 px icons it references, plus the icons themselves. It's the minimum you need for Android home-screen icons and installable progressive web apps.

How to use it

  1. Enter your app name and a short name of 12 characters or less.
  2. Choose theme and background colors.
  3. Pick your icon image and adjust it.
  4. Download the ZIP with the manifest and icons.
  5. Link the manifest from your pages with <link rel="manifest">.

Frequently asked questions

What is a web app manifest?

A JSON file that tells browsers your app's name, icons and colors when it's installed or added to the home screen.

Is it .webmanifest or manifest.json?

Both work. .webmanifest is the official extension; serve it with the application/manifest+json type if your server allows.

Why include a maskable icon?

Android crops icons into different shapes. A maskable icon keeps your logo in the safe center area so it isn't clipped.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
