Block AI Crawlers with Robots.txt (GPTBot & More)

How it works

Generate a robots.txt that blocks AI training and AI search crawlers such as GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, Bytespider, Applebot-Extended and others, while still letting Googlebot and Bingbot crawl your site for search.

How to use it

  1. Start with the AI crawler preset.
  2. Remove any crawlers you want to allow.
  3. Keep the * group open for normal search engines.
  4. Test a URL as GPTBot and as Googlebot.
  5. Download and upload robots.txt to your root.

Frequently asked questions

Does blocking Google-Extended hurt my Google rankings?

No. Google-Extended only controls use of your content for Gemini and AI training; Googlebot crawling for Search is separate.

Do all AI companies respect robots.txt?

The major ones say they do, but robots.txt is voluntary. For stronger protection, block user agents or IP ranges at your server or CDN.

Will this remove my site from AI answers?

It stops future crawling by the listed bots. Content already collected, or quoted by third parties, isn't affected.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
