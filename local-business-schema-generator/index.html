Local Business Schema Generator (JSON-LD)

How it works

Generate LocalBusiness structured data, or a more specific type like Restaurant, Dentist, Store or ProfessionalService, with your name, address, phone, coordinates, price range and opening hours. It helps search engines match your site to your Google Business Profile and local searches.

How to use it

  1. Pick the most specific business type.
  2. Enter your name, address and phone exactly as on your Business Profile.
  3. Add coordinates and opening hours.
  4. Copy the JSON-LD into your home or contact page.
  5. Test it with the Rich Results Test.

Frequently asked questions

Which page should have LocalBusiness schema?

Usually the home page or the contact page. Multi-location businesses should add it to each location page.

What format do opening hours use?

Day ranges and 24-hour times, for example Mo-Fr 09:00-17:00 or Sa 10:00-14:00.

Does it replace a Google Business Profile?

No. Local rankings depend mostly on your Business Profile; schema supports it with consistent details.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
