Robots.txt Generator with Built-in Tester

How it works

Build a robots.txt file from user-agent groups with Allow, Disallow and Crawl-delay rules plus Sitemap lines, or start from a preset: allow everything, block everything, block AI crawlers, a WordPress setup or an online-store setup. A built-in tester checks any URL against your file using Google's longest-match rules.

How to use it

  1. Choose a preset or start with a blank group.
  2. Add user-agent groups and their Allow or Disallow paths.
  3. Add your sitemap URL.
  4. Switch to the tester and check important URLs.
  5. Download robots.txt and upload it to your site's root.

Frequently asked questions

Where does robots.txt go?

At the root of your host, for example https://example.com/robots.txt. Each subdomain needs its own file.

Does Disallow remove a page from Google?

No. It stops crawling, but a blocked URL can still be indexed from links. Use a noindex meta tag on a crawlable page to remove it.

Does Google support Crawl-delay?

No. Google ignores Crawl-delay, but Bing, Yandex and some other crawlers respect it.

Can I use wildcards?

Yes. * matches any sequence of characters and $ anchors the end of the URL, for example Disallow: /*.pdf$.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
