Open Graph Meta Tag Generator with Previews

How it works

Fill in your title, description, URL and image, and Tagloom writes the og: and twitter: meta tags and shows approximate link previews in the styles of Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Slack and Discord. You can pick a local image to see how it crops; it is checked by its file signature and never uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Enter the title, description and page URL.
  2. Paste the absolute URL of your share image (1200 × 630 is ideal).
  3. Optionally choose the same image from your device to preview the crop.
  4. Compare the previews and check the warnings.
  5. Copy the tags into your page's <head>.

Frequently asked questions

What size should an Open Graph image be?

1200 × 630 pixels (a 1.91:1 ratio) works across almost every network. Keep it under 5 MB and keep text away from the edges.

Why doesn't my new image show when I share?

Networks cache previews. Use each network's sharing debugger to refresh the cache after you change your tags.

Do I need both og: and twitter: tags?

X falls back to Open Graph tags, but twitter:card is needed to get the large image layout.

Are these previews exact?

No. They are simplified, generic approximations to help you judge cropping and truncation; real layouts vary by app and device.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
