Netlify _redirects and _headers File Generator

How it works

Create _redirects and _headers files for Netlify and Cloudflare Pages: path redirects with 301, 302, 307, 308 or 410 status codes, a www or non-www domain redirect, a custom 404 rule, long-lived caching for assets and security headers such as HSTS, nosniff and Referrer-Policy.

How to use it

  1. Pick the Netlify / Cloudflare format.
  2. Enter your domain and choose www options.
  3. Add your redirects, one per line.
  4. Turn on caching and security headers.
  5. Save _redirects and _headers in your publish folder and deploy.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cloudflare Pages support _redirects?

Yes, for path redirects. Domain-level redirects on Cloudflare should be set up as Redirect Rules in the dashboard.

What does the ! after 301 mean?

On Netlify, 301! forces the redirect even if a file exists at that path.

Where do the files go?

In the folder your site is published from, such as dist/ or public/, so they're deployed with the site.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Tagloom runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or pick is sent to a server, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
